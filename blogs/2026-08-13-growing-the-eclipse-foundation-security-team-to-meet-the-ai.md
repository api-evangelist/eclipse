Growing the Eclipse Foundation Security Team to Meet the AI Moment For the last several years, the Eclipse Foundation Security Team has worked alongside our project communities to keep the software they produce trustworthy. We facilitate communication between security researchers and maintainers, help evaluate and assess the severity of reported issues, support coordinated disclosure, and advise on fixes and workarounds. As a CVE Numbering Authority for over ten years, we also assign CVE IDs and curate CVE records so the broader ecosystem can rely on accurate, complete vulnerability data.
