Two new research reports examine how open source infrastructure evolves through Open VSX (vendor-neutral extension registry) and Eclipse ThreadX (real-time operating system). Both reports emphasize that sustainable open source requires long-term stewardship, transparent governance, and operational models supporting both communities and commercial adopters.
