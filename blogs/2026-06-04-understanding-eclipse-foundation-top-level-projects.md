This article explains how the Eclipse Foundation organizes hundreds of projects under Top-Level Projects (TLPs), each with its own governance structure. It provides guidance for new proposals, highlighting Eclipse Technology as an ideal home for innovative initiatives that don't fit neatly into existing categories while the community develops stronger ties to specific domains.
