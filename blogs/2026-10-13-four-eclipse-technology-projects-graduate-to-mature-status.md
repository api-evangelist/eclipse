Four Eclipse Technology Projects Graduate to Mature Status We’re pleased to celebrate an important milestone for four projects in the Eclipse Technology Top-Level Project : Eclipse SW360, Eclipse Tycho, Eclipse Apoapsis, and Eclipse Common Security Infrastructure (CSI) . Graduation marks a project’s transition from the incubation phase to the mature phase of the Eclipse Foundation Development Process . It recognises more than the maturity of the technology itself.
