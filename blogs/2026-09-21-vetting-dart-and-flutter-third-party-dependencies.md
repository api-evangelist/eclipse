Vetting Dart and Flutter Third Party Dependencies We’ve recently added support for vetting Dart and Flutter dependencies to IPLab , the service that manages licence data for the Eclipse Dash License Tool . IPLab IPLab is the combination of an issue tracker on the Eclipse Foundation’s GitLab instance and a collection of services that automatically review, manage, and disseminate licence information for third party content leveraged by Eclipse open source projects. The IPLab issue tracker also serves as the Eclipse Foundation’s Intellectual Property Team ’s work queue.
